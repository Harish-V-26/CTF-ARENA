Introduction

How the practical labs work

Welcome to the practical side of CTF LABS! Unlike the beginner quizzes, the Easy Level challenges require you to attack real, running servers. To do this safely, our platform uses Docker to instantly spin up isolated, private lab environments specifically for you.

🐳

Damn Vulnerable Web App (DVWA)

Step-by-step to your first flag

  • Step 1 Launch the Container. Click the red "Launch DVWA Instance" button. Our backend will start a Docker container and give you a unique port. A new tab will open automatically.
  • Step 2 Log in & Setup. Use the default credentials: Username: admin | Password: password. Then, click "Create / Reset Database" at the bottom of the setup page to initialize the app.
  • Step 3 Find the Vulnerability. We need to get the flag! Navigate to the Command Injection tab. This page lets you ping an IP, but it doesn't sanitize input.
  • Step 4 Exploit it. Type 127.0.0.1; ls -la into the box. The semicolon (;) tells Linux to execute a second command after the ping finishes. You should see a list of files.
  • Step 5 Capture the Flag. If you look closely at the files, there isn't a flag.txt in the current directory. However, running commands like 127.0.0.1; cat /etc/passwd proves you have execution. Answer the final questions in the lab correctly to simulate capturing the flag and earning your points!

Do I need to stop my containers?
Our backend automatically shuts down your Docker containers when you click the "Stop" buttons or simply close your browser tab! This ensures your computer doesn't run out of memory.
