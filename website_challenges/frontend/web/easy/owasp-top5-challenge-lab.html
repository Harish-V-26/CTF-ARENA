Mission Briefing

You have discovered a highly secure corporate staging environment hosting multiple internal portals. Your objective is to gain unauthorized access, escalate your privileges, bypass input filters, and extract the secret flag hidden piece by piece.

This is a pure CTF scenario. There are no step-by-step walkthroughs. You must combine the following techniques you have learned in the OWASP Top 10 Part 1 modules:

  • A01: Broken Access Control
  • A02: Cryptographic Failures
  • A03: Injection
  • A05: Security Misconfiguration
  • A08: Software Supply Chain Failures

Each service you compromise will lead you to the next vulnerability in the chain. Collect all flag pieces along your journey and concatenate them together to construct the final flag.

Goal: Submit the complete combined flag, in the format CTF{...}.

Gauntlet Target Server

Launch the vulnerable portal containing all chained OWASP services.
