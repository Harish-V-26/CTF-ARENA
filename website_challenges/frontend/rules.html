Competition Rules

Play Fair. Hack Hard.

Read the rules carefully before participating. Violations can result in disqualification.

Code of Conduct

Expected behavior for all participants

  • #01 Treat all participants with respect. Harassment, discrimination, or abuse of any kind is strictly prohibited.
  • #02 Do not share flags, solutions, or write-ups until the challenge is officially retired.
  • #03 English is the primary language of communication in public channels. Other languages are welcome in private groups.
  • #04 Attacking platform infrastructure (not part of any challenge) is forbidden and will result in immediate disqualification.
  • #05 You must be registered under your real identity. Participants found using fake accounts will be permanently banned.

Flag Submission

How to submit challenge answers correctly

  • #01 All flags follow the format CTF{...} unless the challenge explicitly states otherwise.
  • #02 Flag submission is case-sensitive. Copy the flag exactly as found — do not modify any characters.
  • #03 Brute-forcing the flag submission form is prohibited. After 10 failed attempts, your account will be locked for 15 minutes.
  • #04 Each challenge can only be solved once per account. Submitting a flag you already captured will not award additional points.

Scoring System

How points are calculated and awarded

Difficulty Base Points First Blood Bonus Categories
Easy 75 – 150 pts +50 pts All
Medium 200 – 350 pts +100 pts All
Hard 400 – 600 pts +200 pts All
Insane 700 – 1000 pts +300 pts Special Events

Hint Penalty: Using a hint deducts 10–25% of the challenge base points from your score for that challenge. Hints are optional and cannot be undone.

Team Rules

Guidelines for group participation

  • #01 Teams can have a maximum of 4 members. Solo participation is also permitted.
  • #02 A player may only be a member of one team at a time. Switching teams during an active event is not allowed.
  • #03 Team scores are the sum of all member scores. The leaderboard ranks teams, not individuals, during events.
  • #04 Group lab instances are shared — all team members connect to the same Docker environment simultaneously.

Infrastructure Rules

Responsible use of challenge environments

  • #01 Challenge environments are for your use only. Do not share your lab URL or container credentials.
  • #02 Docker labs auto-terminate after 2 hours. You can restart them, but progress may not persist unless you save your work.
  • #03 Do not attempt to break out of the container or pivot to other platform infrastructure. Scope is limited to the lab environment.
  • #04 Resource-intensive attacks (e.g., massive brute-forcing, DoS) against lab targets are prohibited and may auto-terminate your instance.

Violations & Penalties

Consequences for breaking the rules

  • Flag sharing / spoiling other participants → Score reset + 7-day ban
  • Attacking platform infrastructure → Immediate permanent ban + possible legal action
  • Abuse of hint system / automation bots → Score disqualification for that challenge
  • Harassment or toxic behavior → Warning, then suspension based on severity
  • #01 All disputes must be raised via the official Discord support channel within 24 hours of the incident.
