Introduction

Welcome to Cryptography

Cryptography is the science of keeping information secure. In these beginner labs, you will learn the foundational concepts that power the internet today, from classic ciphers like Caesar and Vigenère to modern systems like AES and RSA.

Classic Ciphers

The origins of encryption

  • Step 1 Caesar Cipher. This is a simple substitution cipher where each letter is shifted by a fixed number of positions down the alphabet. Try shifting "HELLO" by 3 to get "KHOOR".
  • Step 2 ROT13. A special case of the Caesar cipher where the shift is exactly 13. Since there are 26 letters in the alphabet, applying ROT13 twice gets you back to the original text!

Modern Encryption

Symmetric & Asymmetric

  • Step 1 Symmetric Encryption. Uses the same key for both encryption and decryption. Fast and efficient, but requires secure key exchange. AES is the modern standard.
  • Step 2 Asymmetric Encryption. Uses a mathematically linked pair of keys (Public and Private). Anyone can encrypt with the Public key, but only the owner of the Private key can decrypt. RSA and ECC are common examples.
